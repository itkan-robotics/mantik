### Contest Strategies Overview

Contests reward the same coding skills plus discipline: where you spend minutes matters as much as whether you know Dijkstra.

### Key Strategies

- Read all problems first
- Solve easy problems quickly
- Don't get stuck too long
- Manage your time
- Debug efficiently
- Practice under timed conditions

### After the Contest

- Upsolve missed problems
- Analyze mistakes
- Review editorials
- Track your progress
