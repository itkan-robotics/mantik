### Advanced Problem Solving Overview

Hard problems rarely introduce a brand-new algorithm. They stack two or three ideas you already know and hide which stack applies.

### Approach to Advanced Problems

- Break down the problem
- Identify relevant patterns
- Try multiple approaches
- Optimize iteratively
- Don't be afraid to fail and retry

### Practice Resources

- LeetCode Hard problems
- Codeforces Div 1 contests
- AtCoder Grand Contests
- Google Code Jam, Facebook Hacker Cup
